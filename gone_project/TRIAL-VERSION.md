# GONE trial version: notes for testers

Thank you for testing GONE. It is an early version.

- **Your data may be reset** before the full launch. Don't rely on it for anything important yet.
- **Your GONE ID is your login.** Save it and your password somewhere safe. If you forget your password, use the two recovery questions you chose.
- **There is no email or phone number.** GONE never asks for them.
- **Anyone can join.** The home screen shows how many people have a GONE ID.
- **What to test:** sign up, set up your monthly income and one routine reminder, add a few expenses, check Insights and Savings, and tell us anything confusing or broken.
- **Not built yet:** fixed monthly debits that log themselves each month.

Please report bugs with: what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened.
